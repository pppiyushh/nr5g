**Purpose:** Show which wireless companies exist, where they are based and what technology they actually build. This is a technology and industry landscape, not a job-opening tracker.

**Seed coverage:** 41 startups, scale-ups and established companies across 22 countries and six continents. Every row was checked against an official company or product page on 30 August 2026. The selection is intentionally curated, not comprehensive.

**Jump to:** [North America](#north-america) · [Europe](#europe) · [Asia](#asia) · [Oceania](#oceania) · [Africa](#africa) · [South America](#south-america) · [Technology index](#cross-cutting-technology-index)

## How to read the Atlas

- **HQ and footprint** lists headquarters and selected engineering, office, deployment or service locations. It is not a complete legal-entity register.
- **Maturity** follows `Concept → Prototype/evaluation → Field trial → Early commercial → Commercial at scale` when product-level evidence is available.
- A company can be mature in one product and early-stage in another. An established satellite operator can, for example, have a D2D service at field-trial stage.
- “Global” means the company publicly presents global offices, customers, coverage or deployments. It does not mean every product is available in every country.

The placement, evidence and correction rules are documented in the [Atlas methodology]({{ '/atlas/methodology/' | relative_url }}).
