About NR5G

NR5G is a technical knowledge and research platform focused on understanding modern wireless communication systems from both the theoretical and implementation perspectives.


The goal is to bridge the gap between 3GPP specifications, academic research and the software running inside real communication systems.


The platform is maintained by Piyush Kumar Singh, a software engineer working on NR5G Layer-1 modem development.
