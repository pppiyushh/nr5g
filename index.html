About NR5G

NR5G is a technical knowledge and research platform focused on modern wireless communication systems from both theoretical and implementation perspectives.


The goal is to bridge academic research, public standards and the engineering constraints that shape real communication systems.


The platform is maintained by Piyush Kumar Singh, a software engineer working on NR5G Layer-1 modem development.
