## What the Radar covers

- standards and spectrum activity from 3GPP, ITU, ETSI, IEEE, O-RAN and regulators;
- peer-reviewed papers and clearly labelled preprints;
- university laboratories, prototypes, testbeds and field trials;
- wireless startups, products and deployments;
- funding, acquisitions and partnerships as ecosystem signals; and
- patent publications and grants, kept distinct from evidence that a method works.

The selection and verification rules are documented in the [Radar methodology]({{ '/radar/methodology/' | relative_url }}).
