# Cell-Free Networks

## 6G: From Cells to Cell-Free Networks

This article will examine the evolution from conventional macro-cellular networks toward ultra-dense, distributed, user-centric, and cell-free network architectures, with emphasis on the role these concepts may play in 6G.
