## The research question

Current wireless networks already use machine learning for selected optimization problems. The more interesting 6G question is whether AI should remain an auxiliary capability or become a first-class architectural function distributed across the UE, RAN, edge and core.

This article will distinguish three things that are often mixed together:

1. **AI used to optimize an existing network function**
2. **AI-native network functions designed around learning from the beginning**
3. **The proposed idea of an AI plane coordinating models, data, inference, control and lifecycle management across the network**

## Planned investigation

- Why existing control and management mechanisms may not be sufficient for pervasive AI
- AI Plane vs control plane vs user plane
- Where training and inference execute: UE, RAN, edge, core or cloud
- Data collection, model distribution and lifecycle management
- Latency, reliability and energy constraints
- AI for PHY and radio-resource management
- Trust, explainability, security and failure containment
- What is standardized today versus what remains a research proposal
- Open research problems for AI-native 6G
